Privacy Policy Last updated: November 2, 2026 Occasio is designed to be completely private. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, and it does not connect to the internet at all. No Data Collection Occasio has no user accounts and no servers. The developer has no access to anything you enter. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. On-Device Storage The people, occasions, gifts, photos and settings you add are stored only on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). If you use iCloud or Finder/iTunes device backups, this data may be included in your own personal backup under your Apple account; the developer cannot access it. Information About Other People Any details you record about friends or family stay entirely on your device and are never uploaded or shared by the app. You are responsible for the information you choose to store. Camera and Photos Occasio asks for camera access only when you choose to take a photo for a person or gift, and for photo library access only when you choose to pick an existing photo. Photos are stored locally and are never uploaded or shared. You can decline these permissions and still use the app. No Internet Use Occasio makes no network requests. It works fully offline. Children's Privacy Occasio does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Your Control Because nothing is stored off your device, you control all of your data. You can edit or delete anything, use "Erase All Data" in More, or delete the app. Changes If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
